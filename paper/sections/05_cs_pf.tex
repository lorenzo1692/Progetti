\subsection{Axisymmetric field, flux and forces}
Both solvers share an axisymmetric electromagnetic sub-module: field
components of thick coils via complete elliptic integrals and Heuman's
lambda function, vector potential and flux
$\Phi(r,z)=2\pi r A_\theta(r,z)$, self- and mutual inductances, and
Lorentz forces per coil and per scenario time point
\cite{Giannini2023MADE}. This is the sub-module reused for mirrors
(section~\ref{sec:mirror}).

\subsection{Central solenoid}
Input: stack height, outer radius (from the TF inner leg), number of
modules, superconductor option, current and conductor-size ranges, and
the scenario currents of CS, PF and plasma. The search enumerates turns,
layers and current; for each candidate the conductor is sized by the
shared module (hot spot with $\tau=LI/V_\mathrm{max}$), the hoop stress of
the thick solenoid with linear field gradient is recovered on the jacket
through a stiffness-sharing factor, and the vertical stress uses the
axial force crossing the mid-plane computed for each candidate with the
full stack. Fatigue is assessed by fatigue crack growth (Paris law with
ITER criteria, residual stress, Walker correction)
\cite{Giannini2023MADE}. Figures of merit: flux swing vs number of
plasma cycles.
\paragraph{New in 2.0.} Axisymmetric FE model of the full stack (orthotropic
homogenised turns region, inter-module plates, inertial or preloaded
base), loaded by $\mathbf J\times\mathbf B$ for the design case and every
scenario; module-by-module comparison with the analytical stresses;
export to ANSYS. \todo{numbers from the DTT/ITER cases.}

\subsection{Poloidal field coils}
Input: centroid $(R_m,Z_m)$ and maximum ampere-turns of each coil from the
free-boundary equilibrium over the scenario (IMAG, SOF, SOB, EOB), placed
in the domain left free by the optimised TF shape
\cite{Giannini2024MADEIII}. For each coil and WP height the search returns
feasible layouts; the hoop stress uses the thick-ring solution with a
non-zero field at both edges, corrected by
$\gamma=K_\mathrm{eq}(\mathrm{steel})/K_\mathrm{eq}(\mathrm{CICC})$.
Typical figures of merit: minimum peak field (divertor coils, allowing
NbTi) or minimum number of turns / hydraulic length (vertical-field
coils).
\paragraph{New in 2.0.} Scenario forces from the coupling matrix, FCGR as a
sizing option, axisymmetric FE verification of the selected WP.
